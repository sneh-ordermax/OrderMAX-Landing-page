AI recommendations vs. manual upsell rules

A manual upsell rule is a merchant deciding in advance which two products pair — "always show a phone case with this phone." That works until the catalog grows or changes, at which point every rule needs manual upkeep. AI product recommendations instead evaluate the cart's actual contents to choose a relevant add-on, adapting automatically as your catalog and traffic change, without a rule to write for every product.

AI recommendations decide which product to show; that's a different question from which version of the offer converts best (copy, discount, placement) — that's what A/B testing answers. OrderMAX runs both in the same campaign. If your promotion is a gift instead of a paid add-on, see gift with purchase; for a "buy more, get more" mechanic, see BOGO / Buy X Get Y. If a recommendation can land on a cart that already has a code applied, discount stacking covers how to cap the combined cost. Every one of these mechanics is included in the free upsell app — there is no paid tier to unlock them.

Frequently asked questions

What is AI cart upsell?

AI cart upsell is a cart-level offer where the add-on product shown to a shopper is chosen automatically based on what's already in their cart, rather than a single fixed offer set manually for every shopper. The goal is to surface the item most likely to convert for that specific cart.

How do AI product recommendations differ from manual upsell rules?

Manual upsell rules require a merchant to decide in advance which product pairs with which — for example, always showing a phone case with a phone. AI product recommendations instead evaluate the cart's contents (and, where available, catalog and behavior data) to suggest the most relevant add-on, adapting as your catalog changes without needing every rule maintained by hand.

Does AI cart upsell replace A/B testing?

No — they solve different problems. AI recommendations decide which product to show; A/B testing decides which version of the offer (copy, discount, placement) converts best. OrderMAX runs both together: AI-matched recommendations inside a widget that's simultaneously being A/B tested.

Do I need my own product data or AI model to use AI cart upsell?

No. OrderMAX's AI recommendations work from your existing Shopify catalog and campaign setup — there's no separate data pipeline or model to maintain. You launch a campaign through a guided flow and the recommendation logic runs automatically.

Will an AI-recommended upsell look native to my store?

Yes. The recommendation logic decides which product to show; the widget that displays it inherits your store's theme typography, spacing, and button styles automatically, with a design editor for further control.
