GWP vs. a straight discount

A percentage-off discount lowers the price of what a shopper is already buying — it protects nothing on that item's margin. Gift with purchase instead adds something extra at no charge once a threshold is met, which means the core product keeps its full price while shoppers still get a concrete reason to add more to their cart. That's why GWP tends to be used for AOV growth specifically, while blanket discounts are used for clearing inventory or matching a competitor's price.

Free gifts and GWP can also be combined with other promotion types — see BOGO / Buy X Get Y for a related mechanic, or discount stacking if you want to layer a gift trigger on top of a percentage-off code.

Frequently asked questions

What is gift with purchase (GWP)?

Gift with purchase (GWP) is a promotion where a shopper automatically receives a free product once their cart meets a condition — usually a minimum spend, a specific product, or a collection. The gift is added at no cost to the shopper and is designed to increase average order value without discounting the core product.

How is GWP different from a discount?

A discount reduces the price of what the shopper is already buying. GWP adds something extra at no charge instead of lowering price — it protects your margin on the original item while still giving shoppers a reason to spend more to unlock the free product.

How do I set a gift-with-purchase threshold on Shopify?

With OrderMAX, you choose a trigger — cart total, a specific product, or a collection — and pick which product to give away for free once that trigger is met. The gift appears automatically in the cart or via a widget, no code or theme edits required.

Can I run tiered gift-with-purchase offers?

Yes. You can set multiple thresholds so a bigger cart unlocks a better gift — for example a sample at one spend level and a full-size product at a higher one — and track which tier converts best in your funnel analytics.

Does gift with purchase actually increase average order value?

Showing shoppers a free gift they can unlock by adding a set amount to their cart is a well-documented AOV lever in ecommerce, because it gives shoppers a concrete, visible reason to cross a spend threshold rather than checking out with a smaller cart.
