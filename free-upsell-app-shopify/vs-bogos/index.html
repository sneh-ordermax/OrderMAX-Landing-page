OrderMAX vs BOGOS: Free Gift Bundle Upsell

One app's free plan has no ceiling at all. The other's free plan is a one-time allowance of 30 orders — spend it, and the next order needs a paid plan. That single difference in how "free" is defined shapes almost everything else in this comparison.

The short answer

OrderMAX's free plan has no order cap and never expires; BOGOS's free plan is a one-time allowance of 30 lifetime orders, then $29.99–$109.99/month. BOGOS has twelve years of history, 95,000+ brands, and deeper promotional mechanics — GWP, BOGO, bundles, POS and headless support. OrderMAX has one App Store review; BOGOS has 4,092.

How they differ in practice

The pricing table explains the money. It doesn't explain what each app is actually built to do, which is where the two products genuinely diverge — one is a general-purpose upsell app with no cap to manage, the other is a specialist in gift-and-bundle offers with a longer history behind it.

Promotional mechanics depth

BOGOS's stated strength is the deepest promotional mechanics in this comparison: gift-with-purchase, BOGO, bundles, and mix-and-match, all built around the specific mechanics of "give something free with a purchase" rather than a general upsell funnel. Twelve years spent inside that one niche shows up as more configuration options for those specific offer types than a broader upsell app is likely to carry.

Where each app can run

BOGOS supports Shopify POS and headless storefronts — a real differentiator for a merchant running in-store checkout alongside online, or a custom frontend that isn't standard Shopify theme code. That's a verified BOGOS strength; nothing in the verified facts here claims the same for OrderMAX, so a store that specifically needs POS or headless coverage should weigh that gap directly rather than assume parity.

Track record and reviews

95,000+ brands and 4,092 App Store reviews at a 5.0 average is a large, mature sample size — BOGOS has been visible to the Shopify merchant base long enough to accumulate both. OrderMAX has one review. That's not a feature difference, it's an evidence-of-adoption difference, and it's real: a newer app simply has less public track record to check against before you install it.

The honest trade-off

Neither free plan here is free without a catch. The catches are just different shapes.

BOGOS's free plan spends itself once. Thirty lifetime orders is not a monthly allowance that resets — it's a one-time budget. A store doing modest volume might stretch it for weeks; a store doing 30 orders a day burns the entire free tier before lunch on day one and is on a paid plan from that point forward. The free plan is functionally a trial measured in orders instead of days, even though BOGOS doesn't market it that way.

OrderMAX's free plan has no cap, but a short track record. There's no ceiling to hit and no fee to graduate into — the trade a merchant makes instead is choosing an app with a much thinner public review history (one, against BOGOS's 4,092) and no stated POS or headless support to point to. An uncapped free plan doesn't buy the twelve years of edge-case handling that BOGOS's fee revenue and longer history have paid for.

Both trade-offs are real. The right call depends on whether a hard order cap or a thin review history is the bigger risk for a given store.

Where OrderMAX fits

OrderMAX fits a store that wants every upsell and cross-sell feature at $0, with no volume cap to plan around and no upgrade prompt waiting at some order count. That's a straightforward pitch, and it's accurate.

It is not the fit for a store that specifically needs BOGOS's twelve years of gift-and-bundle-specific mechanics, its POS and headless coverage, or the reassurance of a five-figure review count before installing anything. We have one App Store review as of this writing, against BOGOS's 4,092 — a real gap in public track record that a hard cap and a fee schedule don't erase for BOGOS, and that a $0 price tag doesn't erase for us. Read both listings, not just this page, before deciding.

BOGOS, by contrast, fits a store that already knows it wants gift-and-bundle offers specifically, expects to outgrow 30 orders quickly, and would rather pay a known monthly fee for a vendor with twelve years of history than run its early offers on a newer app's single review. Both are legitimate reasons to choose it over OrderMAX, and neither is a knock on the free plan's mechanics.

What the evidence says, and where it stops

Tier 2 — named third party, self-reported. AfterSell's aggregate merchant data reports an average 9.74% AOV lift from post-purchase upsell offers specifically. This is vendor-reported aggregate data, not an independent audit — treat it as directional.

Tier 3 — industry benchmark. McKinsey's widely cited analysis attributes roughly 35% of Amazon purchases to recommendation-driven cross-sells. This describes one platform at extreme scale and personalization depth; it is evidence the mechanism works broadly, not a forecast for any specific Shopify store.

What this evidence does not say: neither figure compares OrderMAX to BOGOS, or a general upsell app to a gift-and-bundle specialist, or a free plan to a paid one. No independent study has tested these two apps head-to-head on matched stores. The category evidence supports "upsells and gift offers generally lift AOV" — it says nothing about which specific app captures more of that lift for your catalogue. That's a question only your own funnel data can answer.

Frequently asked questions

Which is better, OrderMAX or BOGOS: Free Gift Bundle Upsell?

Depends what you weigh. OrderMAX's free plan has no cap and never expires, but carries one App Store review. BOGOS has twelve years in the category, 95,000+ brands, and 4,092 reviews, but its free plan is a one-time 30-order allowance that ends and starts billing at $29.99/mo. Neither wins outright.

How much does BOGOS cost after the free 30 orders run out?

Per BOGOS's own Shopify App Store listing (verified 2026-08-26): Basic is $29.99/mo for 300 orders, Grow is $49.99/mo for 600 orders, Plus is $109.99/mo for 2,000 orders, and orders beyond a plan's cap cost $0.05 each. There is no free tier above the initial 30 lifetime orders.

Does OrderMAX's free plan expire or renew?

Neither, because it has no cap to hit. OrderMAX has one free plan with every feature included and no stated order limit, and there is no paid tier to graduate into. It is the same plan on day one and at any volume afterward.

What does BOGOS do better than OrderMAX?

Three things, by its own track record: twelve years operating in the gift-and-bundle category, 95,000+ brands using it, and support for Shopify POS and headless storefronts. It also runs the deepest promotional mechanics compared here — gift-with-purchase, BOGO, bundles, and mix-and-match in one app.

Is OrderMAX's single App Store review a dealbreaker?

It's a real limitation, not a hidden one. Against BOGOS's 4,092 reviews, OrderMAX has one. That means less public track record to evaluate the app by — weigh it against the free plan's lack of a cap and decide based on your own risk tolerance, not on review count alone.

Does BOGOS support Shopify POS and headless storefronts?

Yes — POS and headless storefront support are among BOGOS's stated strengths, built up over twelve years serving 95,000+ brands. That is a genuine differentiator for any merchant running in-store sales alongside an online store, or a custom/headless frontend.

What happens when a store on BOGOS's free plan hits 30 orders?

The free allowance is lifetime, not monthly, so once those 30 orders are used the store needs a paid plan to keep running offers — starting at $29.99/mo for 300 orders. A store doing 30 orders in its first week exhausts the entire free tier in that week.

Which app has deeper gift-with-purchase and BOGO mechanics?

BOGOS, by its stated strengths — GWP, BOGO, bundles, and mix-and-match are its core, refined over twelve years in the category. That depth is the specific thing a fee buys once a store outgrows BOGOS's 30-order free allowance.

Is a free-forever plan really free, or does it cut features?

For OrderMAX specifically, every feature is included on the free plan with no gated tier above it — there is nothing to unlock by paying, because there is no paid plan. That is a different model from a free plan that samples a fuller paid product.

How was the pricing in this comparison verified?

Every BOGOS figure here — the 30-order free allowance, and the $29.99/$49.99/$109.99 monthly tiers — was read directly from BOGOS's own Shopify App Store listing on 2026-08-26. OrderMAX's pricing is its current published plan. Verify both on the App Store before deciding.
