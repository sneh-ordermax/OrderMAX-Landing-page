How they differ in practice

The plan and pricing table above tells you what each app costs. It doesn't tell you what actually changes for a merchant running one versus the other. Three mechanics do the real work.

AI-matched recommendations vs a flat free tier

Wiser AI's stated strength is AI-driven recommendation matching — the app is built specifically to surface product pairings using a matching model, and that's included from its free plan onward. OrderMAX's free plan doesn't compete on that specific claim; it competes on having no cap and no paid tier at all. If recommendation-matching sophistication is the deciding factor for your catalogue, that's a genuine reason to lean Wiser.

A four-step price ladder vs a single free plan

Wiser AI's pricing steps up gradually: $9/mo at 51 orders, $19/mo at 101, $49/mo at 301. A store crossing the free-tier line doesn't face one large jump to an enterprise price — it faces a small, predictable increase that roughly tracks its own growth. OrderMAX skips this ladder entirely: there's one plan, and it doesn't change as order volume rises.

A monthly-renewing cap vs no cap at all

Wiser AI's free-tier cap is measured in monthly store orders, and it renews every month rather than expiring for good — a slow month can put a store back under 50 orders and back on the free plan the next cycle. That's a materially different mechanic from a lifetime-order cap, which is spent once and never comes back. OrderMAX has no cap of either kind, at any order volume.

The honest trade-off

Wiser AI's downside: a revenue-adjacent cap that expires exactly when the app starts working. A free plan gated at 50 monthly orders is most likely to run out precisely as a store's upsells begin driving real order volume — the app graduates you into a paid tier right at the moment it's proving useful, not before.

OrderMAX's downside: a single App Store review. There is no cap-based catch, but there is a trust catch — a brand-new listing with one public review gives you far less social proof to lean on than an established app with a longer review history. We're stating that plainly rather than burying it, because it's the fact that should actually change your decision if a proven track record matters more to you than an uncapped free plan.

Where OrderMAX fits

OrderMAX fits a store that wants every upsell mechanic — cart offers, cross-sells, gift-with-purchase, BOGO — without a future decision about whether to upgrade hanging over it. There's no order cap to plan around and no second pricing tier waiting once the store grows.

What we're not doing is pretending the review count doesn't matter. One App Store review is a real, current limitation, not a rounding error. If you want to install an app with years of public feedback already attached to it, that's a legitimate reason to pick an established name like Wiser AI instead — and we'd rather you know that going in than find it out later.

What the evidence says, and where it stops

Tier 2 — named third party, self-reported. AfterSell's aggregate merchant data reports an average 9.74% AOV lift from post-purchase upsell offers specifically. This is vendor-reported aggregate data, not an independent audit — treat it as directional.

Tier 3 — industry benchmark. McKinsey's widely cited analysis attributes roughly 35% of Amazon purchases to recommendation-driven cross-sells. This describes one platform at extreme scale and personalization depth; it is evidence the mechanism works broadly, not a forecast for any specific Shopify store.

What this evidence does not say: neither figure isolates OrderMAX or Wiser AI specifically, and no independent study has run the two apps head-to-head on matched Shopify stores. The category evidence supports "upsells generally work" — it does not tell you whether AI-driven matching or an uncapped free plan captures more of that lift for your catalogue. That gap is exactly why the honest answer to "which is better" depends on your own order volume and how much a proven review history is worth to you.

Frequently asked questions

Is OrderMAX or Wiser AI better for a Shopify upsell app?

It depends on what you're optimising for. OrderMAX is free at any order volume with no paid tier, but has a thin App Store review history. Wiser AI offers AI-driven recommendation matching and a gradual four-tier price ladder, but its free plan is capped at 50 orders a month. Neither is universally better.

What does Wiser AI's free plan actually include?

Per Wiser's own Shopify App Store listing (verified 27 August 2026), the free plan is "for stores with 0 to 50 monthly orders." Once a store's monthly order volume exceeds that, paid tiers begin at $9/mo. We haven't independently verified which features are included at each tier — check the listing directly.

Does OrderMAX have an order cap on its free plan?

No stated cap. OrderMAX's free plan includes every feature with no order limit and no paid tier to graduate into, according to its own App Store listing. That's a genuine structural difference from Wiser AI, whose free plan is explicitly bounded by monthly order volume.

What happens when a Wiser AI store passes 50 orders a month?

Based on Wiser's own listing, the store moves into a paid tier: $9/mo for 51–100 orders, $19/mo for 101–300 orders, and $49/mo for 301–500 orders. Each step is a modest increase rather than one large jump, which is one of the two things Wiser does better than a flat free-or-nothing model.

Does Wiser AI's order cap expire permanently once you pass it?

No. The cap is on monthly store orders, and it renews every month rather than expiring for good — a quiet month could put a store back under 50 orders and back on the free plan the next cycle. That's different from a lifetime-order cap, which is spent once and never resets.

Is Wiser AI's recommendation matching better than OrderMAX's?

AI-driven recommendation matching is one of the two things Wiser AI is documented to do better than OrderMAX. We haven't run a head-to-head accuracy test between the two, so treat this as Wiser's stated strength rather than an independently measured win — it's a genuine reason to consider Wiser if matching depth matters most to you.

How many App Store reviews does OrderMAX have?

One, as of this comparison. That's the honest number, and it's thin next to an established app like Wiser AI, which has been on the Shopify App Store far longer. We haven't independently verified Wiser's exact review count here — check both listings yourself before deciding.

Which app should I pick if my store runs under 50 orders a month?

Both are free at that volume, so the decision comes down to two other factors: whether you want AI-driven recommendation matching (Wiser's stated strength) now, or whether you'd rather never think about a cap or an upgrade prompt again (OrderMAX's structural advantage) as your store grows.

Which app should I pick if my store runs over 500 orders a month?

OrderMAX stays free with no cap at any volume. Wiser AI's highest tier verified here tops out at $49/mo for 301–500 orders; pricing above that isn't covered by the facts we verified, so check Wiser's own pricing page directly if your store runs past 500 monthly orders.

Is OrderMAX's free plan a trial, or free forever?

Free forever, not a trial. There is no countdown, no card requirement, and no second pricing tier to graduate into on OrderMAX's App Store listing. That's structurally different from Wiser AI's free plan, which is explicitly scoped as an entry tier beneath three paid tiers, not a permanent ceiling-free plan.
