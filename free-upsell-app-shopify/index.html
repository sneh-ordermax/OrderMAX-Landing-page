What's the catch?

There isn't a hidden fee. There is a real trade-off, and it is more useful to you if we name it.

No revenue share, no per-order fee, no card required. OrderMAX does not take a percentage of upsell-attributed sales, which is how several apps in this category price once you grow. The number does not move with your volume.

Why it's free right now: OrderMAX is early-stage and founder-led. At this stage, merchant adoption and direct product feedback are worth more than subscription revenue. That is a commercial decision, not a favour — it is stated plainly so you can judge it.

What you actually trade: OrderMAX is a newer app with a shorter review history than a decade-old incumbent. You get the complete feature set at no cost; in exchange, you are backing a younger product rather than the safest name in the category. If a long review count is your primary buying criterion, that is a fair reason to choose differently — and worth knowing before you install, not after.

And if pricing ever changes: Shopify apps cannot begin billing a store silently. Any charge has to be approved by you on Shopify's own billing consent screen, and uninstalling is free at any point.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a genuinely free upsell app for Shopify?

Yes, but they are rare. Most apps marketed as free run a capped free plan — a set number of orders or funnel views per month, after which the offers stop or you upgrade. OrderMAX is free in the literal sense: every feature is included at $0, with no order cap, no revenue share and no paid tier to graduate into.

What does OrderMAX include for free?

Everything it does. Adaptive cart upsell and cross-sell widgets, gift-with-purchase campaigns, BOGO / Buy X Get Y offers, AI product recommendations, discount stacking controls, real-time funnel and campaign analytics, automated A/B testing, Shopify Sidekick integration, and founder-led onboarding support. There is no feature held back behind a paid tier.

What is the difference between a free plan and a free trial?

A free trial gives you the full product for a fixed window — usually 7 to 14 days — and then charges the card you entered. A free plan gives you a permanently free but deliberately limited slice of the product, typically capped by monthly orders, funnel views or offer types. A genuinely free app has neither a countdown nor a cap. OrderMAX is the third kind.

Is there an order limit or revenue share on OrderMAX?

No. There is no monthly order cap, no funnel-view cap, and no percentage taken from upsell-attributed revenue. The price is the same whether the app influences ten orders a month or ten thousand.

Why is OrderMAX free — what is the catch?

OrderMAX is early-stage and founder-led, and is prioritising merchant adoption and product feedback over monetisation at this stage. The honest trade-off is not a hidden fee: it is that OrderMAX is a newer app with a smaller review history than a decade-old incumbent. You get the full feature set at no cost; you are betting on a younger product.

What happens if OrderMAX starts charging later?

Any Shopify app charge must be approved by the merchant through Shopify's own billing consent screen — an app cannot begin billing a store silently. If pricing is ever introduced, you would be asked to approve it before any charge, and you can uninstall at any time without penalty.

Do upsells actually increase average order value?

The published evidence consistently points one direction. McKinsey's widely cited analysis attributes roughly 35% of Amazon purchases to recommendation-driven cross-sells. AfterSell's aggregate merchant data reports an average 9.74% AOV lift from post-purchase offers, with 10–20% acceptance rates on well-targeted offers. Individual results vary a great deal by catalog, margin and offer relevance — the mechanism is proven, the magnitude is not guaranteed.

Do free upsell apps slow down your store?

Any app that injects storefront elements adds some weight, free or paid — price is not the variable that determines speed. What matters is whether the widget loads asynchronously, how much script it ships and whether it forces layout shift. Test any upsell app on your own theme with Shopify's speed report and PageSpeed Insights before and after install, rather than trusting a vendor's claim in either direction.

Do I need a developer to install a Shopify upsell app?

Not for OrderMAX. You enable the OrderMAX app block in the Shopify theme editor, then create campaigns through a guided setup flow. Campaigns can be launched, paused and edited without touching theme code.

Will a free upsell widget look native to my theme?

With OrderMAX, yes. Widgets inherit your storefront's typography, spacing and button styling automatically, and a design editor gives you control over layout, colors and copy — no code required. A widget that visibly does not belong to the store is one of the fastest ways to lose the offer.

Can I run gift-with-purchase and BOGO offers on a free app?

On OrderMAX, yes — gift-with-purchase and BOGO / Buy X Get Y are both included at $0, alongside AI-matched cart upsells and discount stacking controls. On most capped free plans, promotional mechanics like GWP and BOGO are among the first features reserved for paid tiers.

How is an upsell app different from Shopify's built-in product recommendations?

Shopify's native recommendations surface related products on the product page. A dedicated upsell app adds offer logic on top: cart and post-purchase placements, conditional triggers, promotional mechanics such as gift-with-purchase and BOGO, per-campaign funnel analytics, and A/B testing of the offer itself. The native feature suggests products; an upsell app runs offers and measures them.

Can I A/B test offers without paying?

On OrderMAX, yes — automated A/B testing is included at $0. Variants of a campaign run side by side and traffic can shift automatically toward the stronger performer once there is enough data. A/B testing is commonly a paid-tier feature elsewhere, which is why many merchants on free plans never learn which offer actually worked.
