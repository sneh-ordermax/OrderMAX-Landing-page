Honeycomb Upsell & Cross Sell vs Wiser AI — Upsell & Cross Sell

Both apps give away a real upsell tool for $0 — but they cap that generosity in two fundamentally different ways, and the difference decides which one survives contact with your actual traffic.

The short answer

Honeycomb's free plan caps at 100 monthly funnel views — consumed by anyone who sees an offer, buyer or not — with a steep $54.99/mo jump to 2,000 views. Wiser AI's free plan instead caps at 0-50 monthly orders, a renewing bracket that steps up gradually: $9, then $19, then $49/mo. Neither is objectively better; each fits a different traffic pattern and budget shape.

How they differ in practice

What actually gets counted against your cap

This is the mechanical difference that matters most. Honeycomb counts funnel views — every time an offer is shown, whether or not the visitor buys, converts, or even scrolls past it. A store running paid traffic or a busy top-of-funnel campaign can chew through 100 views in days without a single extra sale to show for it. Wiser AI counts completed orders instead, which means a slow-converting or high-traffic store isn't penalized for browsing — only for actual sales, and those are the events a merchant wants more of anyway.

A one-time free tier vs. a renewing one

Honeycomb's 100 views are a monthly allowance too, but the jump to the next tier is steep enough that many stores treat hitting the cap as a hard wall rather than a gentle nudge — 55x the price for 20x the views leaves an awkward middle ground with no comparable option. Wiser AI's 0-50 order bracket renews every month at no cost, and its next three tiers step up by roughly 2-2.5x the monthly fee for each 2-3x increase in order allowance, so a store growing steadily has a paid tier sized close to wherever it lands.

Split testing vs. AI matching — two different kinds of "better"

Honeycomb's free plan includes split testing, letting a merchant run two offer variants and see which converts before spending a cent — a feature many competitors, including some paid plans across this category, hold back entirely. Wiser AI's edge is different in kind: AI-driven recommendation matching that aims to surface the right cross-sell per shopper rather than a single static rule. One is a testing tool; the other is a targeting engine. Neither substitutes for the other, and OrderMAX includes neither of these two specific features.

The honest trade-off

Neither app's free tier is a free lunch. Here's what each one actually costs, plainly stated.

Honeycomb's real downside

A funnel-view cap punishes exposure, not just sales — which means the free tier can run out precisely when a store's marketing is working, before a single order has confirmed the offer is worth paying for. And when it does run out, there's no gentle next step: $54.99/mo is the only paid option, a 55x jump that can feel disproportionate for a store that just barely tipped over 100 views.

Wiser AI's real downside

A 50-order free ceiling is generous for a genuinely new store but tight for anything with real traction — a store doing 60-80 orders a month sits just above the free bracket indefinitely, paying $9-19/mo forever for a gap that's barely past the free line. The AI-matching that's the plan's headline advantage is also the one feature least verifiable from a listing page alone; its real quality only shows up once it's running on a live catalogue.

Frequently asked questions

Is Honeycomb or Wiser AI better for a new Shopify store?

Wiser AI's free tier is built for that stage — its 0-to-50-orders bracket exists specifically for low-volume stores. Honeycomb's 100 monthly funnel views can also work early on, but funnel views are consumed by anyone who sees an offer, not just buyers, so a store running any real traffic burns through them faster than order count alone would suggest.

What is the actual free plan cap on Honeycomb Upsell & Cross Sell?

As of 26 August 2026, Honeycomb's own Shopify App Store listing states its free plan includes 100 monthly funnel views. A funnel view is counted whenever anyone sees an offer, whether or not they buy, so it caps exposure rather than sales.

What is the actual free plan cap on Wiser AI — Upsell & Cross Sell?

As of 27 August 2026, Wiser AI's own Shopify App Store listing states the free plan is for stores with 0 to 50 monthly orders. The cap is tied to store order volume and renews every month rather than expiring permanently.

Why does Honeycomb's first paid tier cost so much more than Wiser AI's?

Honeycomb's cheapest paid plan is $54.99/mo for 2,000 views, a 55x jump from its free tier's 100 views. Wiser AI instead steps up gradually: $9/mo for 51-100 orders, $19/mo for 101-300, and $49/mo for 301-500. One vendor front-loads the jump; the other spreads it across tiers.

Does Honeycomb's free plan include split testing?

Yes. Split testing on the free tier is Honeycomb's standout advantage in this comparison — most competitors, including many paid plans across the category, reserve A/B testing for a paying tier. It lets a free-tier merchant validate offer variants before any revenue commitment.

Does a Wiser AI free plan expire once a store outgrows 50 orders?

Not permanently. The 50-order cap renews every month rather than being a lifetime allowance. A store that does 60 orders one month and 40 the next stays inside the free bracket in the second month — the cap tracks monthly order volume, not a cumulative total.

Which app has more Shopify App Store reviews?

Honeycomb Upsell & Cross Sell shows 4.6 stars across 146 reviews on its own listing. Wiser AI — Upsell & Cross Sell showed 4.9 stars across 513 reviews as fetched live from its own listing on 27 August 2026 — a larger review base, though rating snapshots shift over time and should be checked against the live listing before deciding.

Is there a free upsell app with no cap that expires or renews at all?

OrderMAX is a third option worth knowing about here, on ordermax.io, our own site: no funnel-view cap and no order-volume cap on its free plan. It's one option among several, not a neutral pick, and it doesn't include Honeycomb's split testing or Wiser AI's AI-matched recommendations.

Do funnel-view caps or order-volume caps run out faster?

It depends on traffic quality, not just sales. A store with a lot of browsing but few purchases burns through a funnel-view cap like Honeycomb's fast, since views count regardless of conversion. A store with low traffic but a decent conversion rate stretches an order-volume cap like Wiser AI's further, since only completed orders count.

Can I switch from Honeycomb or Wiser AI to another upsell app later?

Yes. Upsell apps in this category generally install as a Shopify app extension without touching your theme's core files, so switching mainly means turning one off and configuring another. Confirm each app's own uninstall notes before switching, since cleanup steps vary by vendor.
