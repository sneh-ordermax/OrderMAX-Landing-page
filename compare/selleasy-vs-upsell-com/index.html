Selleasy (Upsell & Cross Sell) vs Upsell.com (formerly ReConvert)

One free plan is capped only by your store's own order count and renews forever. The other's free plan can't run on a live store at all — it exists purely for development testing. That single gap, not the feature lists, is what actually separates these two apps.

The short answer

Selleasy's free plan is usable on a live store — up to 50 orders a month, renewing monthly, forever. Upsell.com's free plan only works on development stores, so on a real live store its cheapest working plan is $4.99/mo. Selleasy is the better $0 option; Upsell.com is cheaper once you're paying anyway.

How they differ in practice

The pricing tables tell you what each plan costs. They don't tell you what the apps are actually built to do — and on that, Selleasy and Upsell.com aren't really solving the same problem.

Where the free tier actually lives

Selleasy's free plan runs on your real storefront, taking real orders, capped only by your store's own monthly order count. Upsell.com's free plan runs on a Shopify development store — a sandbox environment used for building and testing themes or apps before launch, not a channel that ever sees a live customer. That means Upsell.com effectively has no free plan for any operating business; its real starting price is $4.99/mo.

Recommendation breadth vs. full-funnel depth

Selleasy's edge, per its own listing, is frequently-bought-together recommendations spread across the product page, the cart, and post-purchase — three placements pulling from the same matching logic. Upsell.com's edge is narrower and deeper: full-stack post-purchase and thank-you-page funnels, with A/B testing and advanced analytics built in from its very first paid tier. A store optimizing what happens right after checkout gets more purpose-built tooling from Upsell.com; a store wanting broad cross-sell coverage across the whole shopping journey gets more from Selleasy.

Feature parity vs. volume-gated features

Upsell.com's listing describes A/B testing and advanced analytics as identical across every tier — the gate is volume (orders), not functionality. Selleasy's listing doesn't make the same feature-parity claim across its tiers; its own emphasis is on broad international support once you're past the free cap. That's a meaningfully different pricing philosophy: pay for more orders on Upsell.com, or pay for more orders and possibly more capability on Selleasy.

The honest trade-off

Neither app's free plan is free of a real cost. Both vendors have simply chosen to charge it in a different currency than dollars.

Selleasy's real downside: the cap is your whole store, not just the upsell

Selleasy's free plan counts your store's total order count, not orders the upsell app itself influenced. A store can lose its free plan from general sales growth that has nothing to do with Selleasy at all — the app could be doing almost nothing and you'd still cross 50 orders a month simply because the rest of the business is doing well. The cap renews every month, which is generous, but it's tied to a number the app doesn't control.

Upsell.com's real downside: the free tier expires the moment you go live

A development-store-only free plan isn't a capped allowance you can spend down over time — it's a plan that never applies to a real store in the first place. There's no free orders to burn through, no monthly reset to wait for, because the free tier was never usable for actual sales. For any merchant running a live storefront, Upsell.com's honest starting price is $4.99/mo from day one, not $0.

What the evidence says, and where it stops

Tier 2 — named third party, self-reported. AfterSell's aggregate merchant data reports an average 9.74% AOV lift from post-purchase upsell offers specifically. This is vendor-reported aggregate data, not an independent audit — treat it as directional.

Tier 3 — industry benchmark. McKinsey's widely cited analysis attributes roughly 35% of Amazon purchases to recommendation-driven cross-sells. What this evidence does not say: neither figure was measured on Selleasy or Upsell.com specifically, and no independent study has run the two apps head-to-head on matched Shopify stores. The category evidence supports "cross-sell and post-purchase mechanics generally lift AOV" — it says nothing about which of these two apps lifts it more for your catalogue, or whether Upsell.com's A/B testing actually outperforms Selleasy's broader placement coverage on your specific traffic.

Frequently asked questions

Which is better, Selleasy or Upsell.com?

Neither wins outright. Selleasy's free plan works on a live store today, capped at 50 orders a month and renewing every month. Upsell.com's free plan only runs on development stores, so its cheapest live-store option is $4.99/mo — but that tier includes full post-purchase funnels and A/B testing Selleasy doesn't emphasize.

Can I actually use Upsell.com's free plan on my live Shopify store?

No. As of 26 August 2026, Upsell.com's own App Store listing states its free plan is "Available for development stores" only. A development store is a testing environment, not a real storefront taking customer orders — so for any live business, Upsell.com's real starting price is $4.99/mo, not $0.

What does Selleasy's free plan actually include?

Per Selleasy's own listing, verified 26 August 2026: "Up to 50 orders/mo. Your store's total order count will be considered." That's your entire store's order volume, not just orders the upsell influenced, and the allowance renews every month rather than expiring for good.

How much does each app cost after the free plan?

Selleasy's paid tier starts at $9/mo once a store passes 50 total orders in a month. Upsell.com's paid tier starts at $4.99/mo from 50 orders — the cheaper of the two entry prices, despite Upsell.com bundling deeper post-purchase funnel features from that tier up.

How many reviews do Selleasy and Upsell.com have?

As of 26 August 2026, Selleasy (Upsell & Cross Sell) shows 4.9 stars across 2,525 reviews on the Shopify App Store. Upsell.com (formerly ReConvert) shows 4.8 stars across 2,803 reviews. Both are well-established, high-volume listings in the upsell and cross-sell category.

What is Upsell.com's real advantage over Selleasy?

Full-stack post-purchase and thank-you-page funnels, plus A/B testing and advanced analytics that stay identical across every paid tier. Selleasy's strength runs the other way: frequently-bought-together recommendations across the product page, cart, and post-purchase, with broad international support — breadth over funnel-testing depth.

Is Selleasy or Upsell.com better for a high-volume store?

For staying on a working free plan, Selleasy — its 50-orders-a-month cap renews indefinitely at $0 and works on a live store from day one. Upsell.com's free tier never applies to a live store regardless of volume, so a growing store on Upsell.com pays from $4.99/mo the moment it goes live.

Which app is better for post-purchase upsells specifically?

Upsell.com, based on its own listing's emphasis on full-stack post-purchase and thank-you-page funnels with built-in A/B testing and analytics from its paid tiers. Selleasy also supports post-purchase placements within its broader recommendation engine, but its documented strength is cross-sell breadth, not funnel-testing depth.
