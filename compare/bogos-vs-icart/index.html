BOGOS: Free Gift Bundle Upsell vs iCart Cart Drawer Cart Upsell

One app has spent twelve years refining gift-with-purchase, BOGO, and bundle logic across 95,000+ stores. The other rebuilt the cart drawer itself. Their free plans don't even cap the same way you'd assume — this is where the two actually pull apart, not on marketing copy.

The short answer

BOGOS wins on depth — GWP, BOGO, and bundle logic refined over twelve years across 95,000+ stores, with POS and headless support. iCart wins on the cart itself — it replaces the drawer with built-in progress bars and countdown timers. BOGOS's free plan lasts 30 lifetime orders; iCart's lasts 100 lifetime orders.

Promotional depth: BOGOS's biggest edge

BOGOS (formerly Free Gifts by Secomapp) has been building exactly one category of mechanic for twelve years: gift-with-purchase, BOGO, bundles, and mix-and-match offers. That focus shows up as breadth — 95,000+ brands have run promotions through it, and its App Store listing is the only one of the two here that claims both POS and headless storefront support. For a merchant running layered gift thresholds ("spend $75, get a free gift; spend $150, pick from three") or complex mix-and-match bundle logic, that's twelve years of edge cases already handled, not a feature list to take on faith.

iCart's listing doesn't make competing claims in this specific area — its cart-drawer rebuild is a different kind of app, aimed at a different mechanic entirely.

The cart drawer itself: iCart's biggest edge

Most upsell apps — BOGOS included — inject offers into whatever cart drawer the store's theme already renders. iCart takes a different approach: it replaces the cart drawer itself. Progress bars toward a free-shipping threshold, the threshold logic, and countdown timers are built into that replacement drawer natively, rather than layered on top as a widget. That's a meaningfully different integration point — it's the one thing in this comparison neither BOGOS's listing nor the wider category claims to do the same way.

The trade-off is scope, not quality: iCart's edge is specifically the cart experience. It isn't positioned as a gift-and-bundle engine, and its App Store listing doesn't claim BOGOS's POS or headless coverage.

Free-tier runway: 30 orders vs 100

Both free plans are lifetime allowances, not monthly quotas — but they're not the same size. iCart's "first 100 orders free for lifetime" gives a new store more than three times the runway of BOGOS's "30 free orders lifetime." For a store just starting to test upsell mechanics, that difference alone can decide which app survives long enough to produce a real read on performance before a bill shows up.

What happens after the cap is spent differs too: BOGOS's next step is a $29.99/mo Basic plan, a real jump from $0. iCart's next step is $9/mo — a smaller gap to cross, though it also buys a smaller order allowance (101–200 orders) than BOGOS's Basic tier (300 orders).

The honest trade-off with both

Neither app's free plan is a trap, but neither is free forever, and both downsides are worth stating plainly rather than glossing over.

BOGOS's downside: a 30-order lifetime cap is genuinely small. A store doing even modest volume can burn through it in days, not months, and because it's lifetime rather than monthly, it doesn't refresh — the free tier is spent once, permanently, and the next step up is a real $29.99/mo commitment with no smaller paid rung in between.

iCart's downside: the 100-order lifetime cap is larger but shares the same non-renewing structure, and its first paid tier tops out at just 200 orders for $9/mo — a store growing past that has to keep climbing tiers relatively fast. Replacing the cart drawer itself is also a deeper integration point than injecting offers into an existing one, which is worth testing carefully against your theme before committing to it as your cart's foundation.

Neither trade-off is disqualifying. They're the reason "which is free" is the wrong first question — "which cap and which mechanic matches what I'm actually trying to run" is the right one.

Frequently asked questions

Which is better, BOGOS or iCart?

Neither wins outright — they solve different problems. BOGOS is the deeper choice for gift-with-purchase, BOGO, and bundle promotions after twelve years building that specific mechanic. iCart is the deeper choice if you want the cart drawer itself rebuilt with progress bars and countdown timers. Pick by mechanic, not by a single "better" verdict.

How many free orders does BOGOS give you?

As of 26 August 2026, BOGOS's own Shopify App Store listing states "30 free orders lifetime by BOGOS." That is a one-time lifetime allowance, not a monthly renewing quota — once a store processes 30 orders through the app, the free tier is spent for good, not refreshed the next month.

How many free orders does iCart give you?

As of 26 August 2026, iCart's own Shopify App Store listing states "First 100 orders free for lifetime." Like BOGOS, this is a one-time lifetime cap rather than a monthly allowance — it's more than triple BOGOS's 30-order free tier, giving a new store more runway before it needs to pay.

What happens after BOGOS's free plan runs out?

The next tier is Basic at $29.99/mo for 300 orders, then Grow at $49.99/mo for 600, then Plus at $109.99/mo for 2,000, with $0.05 charged per order beyond a plan's cap. There's a real jump from $0 to $29.99/mo — there is no small paid step in between.

What happens after iCart's free plan runs out?

iCart's first paid tier is $9/mo, covering 101–200 orders. That's a considerably smaller jump than BOGOS's move to $29.99/mo, which matters most for a store that has outgrown its lifetime free orders but isn't yet doing meaningful monthly volume.

What does BOGOS do that iCart doesn't?

BOGOS has twelve years in the gift-and-bundle category, 95,000+ brands using it, and support for both POS and headless storefronts — plus the deepest promotional mechanics compared here: gift-with-purchase, BOGO, bundles, and mix-and-match. iCart's listing does not claim POS or headless support.

What does iCart do that BOGOS doesn't?

iCart is the only app in this comparison that replaces the cart drawer itself, rather than injecting offers into the existing one. That means progress bars, free-shipping thresholds, and countdown timers are built into the cart experience natively, not layered on top of it.

Are BOGOS and iCart rated differently on the Shopify App Store?

Yes. BOGOS carries a 5.0 rating across 4,092 reviews; iCart carries a 4.7 rating across 434 reviews. BOGOS has roughly ten times the review volume, consistent with its twelve years in the category against iCart's shorter track record.

Can I use either app's free plan on a live store indefinitely?

Only until you hit the lifetime cap — 30 orders for BOGOS, 100 for iCart. Neither free plan is a dev-store-only sandbox; both process real orders on a live store. But because the cap is lifetime rather than monthly, a growing store will exhaust it permanently, not just for that billing cycle.

It doesn't match BOGOS's promotional depth or iCart's cart-drawer rebuild; it's a different trade-off, not a strict upgrade over either.
