Selleasy (Upsell & Cross Sell) vs BOGOS: Free Gift Bundle Upsell

Two well-reviewed Shopify apps, two completely different free-plan mechanics. One renews every month; the other spends itself once and never comes back. That single difference — not the feature lists — is what actually decides which one survives contact with a growing store.

The short answer

Selleasy's free plan resets every month, capped at 50 total store orders. BOGOS's free plan is 30 orders, once, for the life of the store — after that, free is gone permanently. Selleasy is the better long-term free option; BOGOS wins on promotional depth (GWP, BOGO, mix-and-match) once you're paying.

How they differ in practice

The pricing tables tell you what each plan costs. They don't tell you what the apps are actually built to do — and on that, Selleasy and BOGOS aren't really competing for the same job.

Recommendation breadth vs. promotional depth

Selleasy's edge, per its own listing, is frequently-bought-together recommendations that show up across the product page, the cart, and post-purchase — three separate placements pulling from the same matching logic. BOGOS's edge is the opposite kind of depth: gift-with-purchase, buy-one-get-one, bundles, and mix-and-match offers, the promotional mechanics a pure recommendation engine typically doesn't build. A store running "spend $75, get a free gift" needs BOGOS's mechanic; a store trying to surface "customers who bought this also bought" needs Selleasy's.

Sales channel reach: storefront-only vs. POS and headless

BOGOS's listing specifically calls out POS and headless storefront support — relevant to any brand selling in person or through a custom-built front end, where a purely storefront-embedded upsell widget won't render at all. Selleasy's listing emphasizes broad international support instead: language and market coverage for a store selling across borders through the standard online storefront. Neither claim is about the same axis, which is exactly why they aren't directly comparable line items.

Track record: twelve years of gifting mechanics vs. a broad cross-sell toolkit

Before the BOGOS rebrand, this app ran as Free Gifts by Secomapp — twelve years now spent on one mechanic, gift-and-bundle promotions, currently used by more than 95,000 brands. Selleasy built its track record differently, spreading a single recommendation engine across three placements (product, cart, post-purchase) instead of going deep on one promotional category the way BOGOS has.

The honest trade-off

Neither free plan is free of a real cost. Both vendors have simply chosen to charge it in a different currency than dollars.

Selleasy's real downside: the cap is your whole store, not just the upsell

Selleasy's free plan counts your store's total order count, not orders the upsell app itself influenced. That means a store can lose its free plan purely from general sales growth that has nothing to do with Selleasy at all — the app can be sitting there doing almost nothing and still push you over 50 orders a month simply because the rest of the business is doing well. The cap renews every month, which is generous, but it's tied to a number the app doesn't control.

BOGOS's real downside: the free plan spends itself once, permanently

Thirty orders sounds generous until you notice it's a one-time budget, not a monthly one — a moderately active store can burn through it in about a week, with no reset waiting next billing cycle. From there, the only way to keep using the app at all is a paid plan starting at $29.99/mo; there's no smaller free tier to fall back into. Set against Selleasy's free plan, which renews on its own every month, BOGOS's version functions less like an ongoing free tier and more like a bounded trial that happens to share the word "free."

Tier 3 — industry benchmark. McKinsey's widely cited analysis attributes roughly 35% of Amazon purchases to recommendation-driven cross-sells. What this evidence does not say: neither figure was measured on Selleasy or BOGOS specifically, and no independent study has run the two apps head-to-head on matched Shopify stores. The category evidence supports "upsell and gifting mechanics generally lift AOV" — it says nothing about which of these two apps lifts it more for your catalogue. That gap is exactly why the free plan of whichever app fits your use case is worth testing before either store pays for anything.

Frequently asked questions

Which is better, Selleasy or BOGOS?

Neither wins outright — they solve different problems. Selleasy is stronger for frequently-bought-together recommendations across product, cart, and post-purchase, with broad international support. BOGOS is stronger for gift-with-purchase, BOGO, and bundle promotions, plus POS and headless storefront support. The right pick depends on which mechanic your store actually needs.

What's the difference between Selleasy's and BOGOS's free plans?

Selleasy's free plan allows up to 50 orders per month, counting your store's total order volume, and it renews every month indefinitely. BOGOS's free plan allows 30 orders total, for the lifetime of the store — once used, that allowance never comes back, free or paid.

Does BOGOS's free plan renew every month?

No — BOGOS's own App Store listing, checked 26 August 2026, grants "30 free orders lifetime": a one-time allowance for the life of the store, not a quota that comes back next billing cycle. Selleasy takes the opposite approach, renewing its 50-order allowance every single month.

How many reviews do Selleasy and BOGOS have?

As of 26 August 2026, Selleasy (Upsell & Cross Sell) shows 4.9 stars across 2,525 reviews on the Shopify App Store. BOGOS: Free Gift Bundle Upsell shows 5.0 stars across 4,092 reviews. Both are well-established, high-volume listings in the upsell category.

What does BOGOS cost after the free plan?

BOGOS's paid tiers, per its own listing, are $29.99/mo for the Basic plan (300 orders), $49.99/mo for Grow (600 orders), and $109.99/mo for Plus (2,000 orders), with a $0.05 charge per order beyond your plan's cap.

What does Selleasy cost after the free plan?

Selleasy's paid tier starts at $9/mo once a store passes 50 total orders in a month, per its own listing. That fee unlocks continued use above the free cap; Selleasy's listing does not break out further tiers beyond that starting price.

Which app is better for gift-with-purchase or BOGO promotions?

BOGOS. Gift-with-purchase, buy-one-get-one, bundles, and mix-and-match are its core specialty, built up over twelve years in the category. Selleasy is built around product recommendations and cross-sells, not promotional gifting mechanics, so it isn't the natural fit for GWP-style offers.

Which app is better for a store on Shopify POS?

BOGOS, based on the facts each vendor publishes — its listing specifically calls out POS and headless storefront support. Selleasy's own listing does not make the same claim, so a store selling in-person through POS has more documented reason to lean toward BOGOS.

Is Selleasy or BOGOS better for a high-volume store?

On free plans specifically, Selleasy tends to suit ongoing volume better, since its 50-orders-a-month cap renews indefinitely at $0. BOGOS's 30-order lifetime cap is exhausted almost immediately at real volume, pushing a growing store into a paid tier much sooner.
