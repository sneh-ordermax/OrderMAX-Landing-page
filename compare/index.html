Frequently asked questions

How was this comparison hub built?

Why do some of these apps not have a real free plan?

Two apps in this set — Upsell.com and, in a milder way, most capped competitors — advertise "free" in a way that doesn't hold up on inspection. Upsell.com's free tier is development-stores-only and cannot run on a live storefront at all; most others cap a genuine free tier by orders, revenue, or funnel views rather than offering it unconditionally.

What's the difference between a lifetime cap and a monthly cap?

A monthly cap (Selleasy, Wiser AI) renews every billing cycle — a store that drops below the threshold stays free indefinitely. A lifetime cap (iCart, BOGOS) is a one-time allowance that, once spent, never resets; it functions closer to an extended trial than an ongoing free plan.
