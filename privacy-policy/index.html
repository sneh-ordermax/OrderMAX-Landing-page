1. Who we are

OrderMAX ("OrderMAX", "we", "us", or "our") builds a cart upsell and cross-sell app for Shopify merchants. Our business address is Manhattan, New York, NY, United States.

For visitors in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, OrderMAX is the data controller for personal information collected through this website.

2. What this policy covers

This policy applies to this marketing website and to enquiries you send us through it — for example, when you book a call with us.

It does not cover:

  • The OrderMAX Shopify app installed in a merchant's store. Data handling there is described in section 13 and in the app's listing on the Shopify App Store.
  • Third-party websites we link to, such as the Shopify App Store or our scheduling provider. Those services have their own privacy policies.
  • Shoppers browsing a merchant's storefront. If you are a shopper, the merchant whose store you visited is the controller of your data — please contact them.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information you give us

  • Booking a call. If you schedule a call through our scheduling link, you provide your name, email address, chosen time, and any notes you add. This is collected and processed by Cal.com on our behalf.
  • Direct correspondence. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message.

3.2 Information collected automatically

When you browse this website, we collect limited technical and usage information through Google Analytics 4:

  • Device and browser data — browser type, operating system, screen size, language, and approximate location derived from your IP address (city or region level; Google Analytics does not log your full IP address).
  • Usage data — pages viewed, time on page, scroll depth, referring website, and interactions with page elements such as opening an FAQ, using the revenue calculator, or clicking a call-to-action.
  • Identifiers — a randomly generated analytics identifier stored in a cookie so repeat visits can be recognised as the same session or browser.

We do not ask for or intentionally collect sensitive personal information on this website, and we do not require an account to browse it. The demo cart and revenue calculator on our homepage run entirely in your browser — the numbers you enter are not sent to us or stored, although the fact that you used those tools is recorded as an analytics event.

If you are in the EEA or UK, we rely on the following legal bases under the GDPR and UK GDPR:

  • Consent — for analytics cookies and similar technologies, where consent is required. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests — to operate, secure, and improve our website and to respond to enquiries you initiate, balanced against your rights and expectations.
  • Performance of a contract or steps prior to entering one — where you contact us about using OrderMAX.
  • Legal obligation — where retention or disclosure is required by law.

9. How long we keep information

  • Analytics data — retained in Google Analytics for up to 14 months, after which event-level data is deleted; aggregate reporting may persist.
  • Booking and enquiry records — kept for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and for a reasonable period afterwards for our business records, generally no more than 24 months unless a longer period is required by law.
  • Server and security logs — typically retained for a short period, generally under 90 days.

10. How we protect information

This website is served over HTTPS, and access to any information we hold is limited to people who need it to do their job. We use reputable providers with their own security programmes. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security — but we work to keep the amount of information we hold small, which is the most effective protection of all.

12. Children's privacy

This website is intended for business audiences and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

13. The OrderMAX Shopify app

Installing the OrderMAX app from the Shopify App Store is separate from visiting this website. When a merchant installs the app, OrderMAX processes store data — such as product catalogue, cart and order information, and campaign performance data — in order to display offers and produce analytics. In that context OrderMAX acts as a processor on behalf of the merchant, who is the controller of their shoppers' data.

The app's data access, retention, and deletion practices are described in its Shopify App Store listing and in the agreement entered into at install time, and they follow Shopify's protected customer data requirements. Merchants can revoke all access by uninstalling the app; associated data is deleted in line with Shopify's mandatory data-deletion webhooks. If you are a shopper who saw an OrderMAX offer in a store, please direct requests to that merchant.
