Best free upsell apps for Shopify, compared

Seven upsell apps advertise a free plan. They are capped in four different units — total store orders, lifetime orders, attributed revenue and funnel views — which is why no two roundups agree on which is most generous. Every figure below was read from that app’s own Shopify App Store listing on the same day and quoted verbatim.

The short answer

Six of the seven free plans are capped. Selleasy is the most generous cap for a low-volume store at 50 orders a month, though it counts your total store orders rather than upsell orders. Essential Upsell ties its cap to $100 of attributed revenue, so it only expires once the app has paid for itself. iCart and BOGOS give lifetime order allowances (100 and 30) that function as extended trials. Honeycomb allows 100 funnel views a month and then jumps to $54.99. Upsell.com’s free plan is development-stores-only and cannot be used on a live storefront. OrderMAX — our own app, disclosed below — states no cap, and has one App Store review against competitors with thousands.

Selleasy — the most generous ongoing free tier, with one widely misread condition

4.9 · 2,525 reviews · Logbase

Selleasy is the strongest all-round free option here for a genuinely low-volume store. Its free tier is not a lifetime allowance that runs out — it renews monthly, and a store under the threshold can stay on it indefinitely with all upsell and cross-sell features available.

The condition merchants miss: the listing states “Up to 50 orders/mo. Your store’s total order count will be considered.” That is total store orders, not orders the app influenced. A store doing 60 orders a month pays $9 whether Selleasy touched one of them or none. Read as an app fee rather than an upsell fee, it is still inexpensive; read as “free until the app makes me money,” it is wrong.

Essential Upsell & Cross Sell — the only cap that waits until the app has paid for itself

5.0 · 2,234 reviews · Essential Bundles & Upsells

Essential ties its free tier to outcome rather than effort: “Up to $100 additional revenue generated.” Structurally this is the fairest capped plan in the comparison, because it cannot expire before the app has demonstrably produced something. What this comparison does not tell you

Four things it deliberately does not claim, because the evidence for them does not exist:

It does not rank the apps by performance. No one has run these seven apps against the same catalogue, traffic and offer strategy. Any roundup claiming one lifts AOV more than another is comparing vendor marketing, not results. The published category evidence — McKinsey’s estimate that roughly 35% of Amazon purchases come from recommendation-driven cross-sells, AfterSell’s reported 9.74% average AOV lift from post-purchase offers — says the mechanism works. It says nothing about which app implements it best.

It does not compare storefront speed. Any app injecting storefront elements adds weight, free or paid; price is not the variable. Test on your own theme with Shopify’s speed report before and after install.

It does not verify feature parity. Two apps both listing “AI recommendations” may mean very different things by it. The caps were verifiable in an afternoon; the feature claims were not.

Pricing goes stale. Everything here was read on 26 August 2026. App Store pricing changes without notice, and at least two apps on this page have restructured their tiers within the last two years. Check the listing before you install.

Frequently asked questions

Which Shopify upsell apps have a genuinely free plan?

As of 26 August 2026, seven widely used upsell apps advertise a free tier: OrderMAX, Selleasy, Essential Upsell & Cross Sell, BOGOS, iCart, Upsell.com (ReConvert) and Honeycomb. Six of the seven cap the free tier. OrderMAX is the only one of the seven with no stated cap, and Upsell.com’s free plan is restricted to development stores, so it is not usable on a live storefront.

What actually ends a free upsell app plan?

Four different units, which is why roundups disagree. Selleasy counts your store’s total monthly orders, not upsell orders, and charges above 50 per month. iCart and BOGOS count lifetime orders — 100 and 30 respectively — after which the free tier is spent permanently. Essential Upsell counts attributed revenue and stops at $100 generated. Honeycomb counts funnel views and allows 100 per month. Comparing the headline word “free” across these is meaningless; comparing the unit is not.

Is Selleasy’s free plan really 50 orders?

Yes, but the count is your store’s total order volume, not the orders the app influenced. Selleasy’s own listing states: “Up to 50 orders/mo. Your store’s total order count will be considered.” A store doing 60 orders a month pays $9 even if the app touched none of them. This is the single most misread free plan in the category.

Which free upsell app is best for gift with purchase and BOGO?

For depth of promotional mechanics, BOGOS is the most established — twelve years in the category, 95,000+ brands, and support for POS and headless storefronts. Its free tier is the tightest in this comparison at 30 lifetime orders, so it functions as an extended trial rather than an ongoing free plan. For gift-with-purchase and BOGO on an uncapped free tier, OrderMAX includes both, with the trade-off that it is a much newer app.

Is a free upsell app good enough for a real store?

For validating whether upsells lift your average order value, yes — every app in this comparison can run a real offer on a real storefront. The question is what happens after validation. On a capped plan the offers stop or the bill starts precisely when the app begins working, which is why the cap unit matters more than the feature list when you choose.

How many reviews does OrderMAX have?

One, rated 5.0, as of 26 August 2026. Every other app in this comparison has between 146 and 4,092 reviews. A merchant choosing an upsell app on track record rather than on pricing should weigh that heavily, and this comparison does not argue otherwise.

What is the difference between a free plan and a free trial on the Shopify App Store?

A free trial gives the full product for a fixed window — commonly 7 to 30 days in this category — and then bills the store. A free plan is permanently available but deliberately limited by a cap. Several apps run both: Selleasy pairs a free-to-install tier with 30-day trials on its paid tiers, and Upsell.com pairs a development-store-only free plan with 14-day trials. Shopify requires merchant approval through its own billing screen before any charge begins, so no app can start billing silently.

Do upsells actually increase average order value?

The mechanism is well evidenced; the magnitude is not guaranteed. McKinsey’s widely cited analysis attributes roughly 35% of Amazon purchases to recommendation-driven cross-sells, and AfterSell’s aggregate merchant data reports an average 9.74% AOV lift from post-purchase offers. Neither figure predicts a specific store’s result, which depends on catalogue, margin and offer relevance. That uncertainty is the strongest argument for starting on a free tier rather than a paid one.
